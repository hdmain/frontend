# AlfaHost

> EU hosting for game servers, Discord bots, NAT VPS and KVM VPS. Anti-DDoS included on every plan. Order in the client panel at https://cp.alfahost.eu.

AlfaHost (alfahost.eu) sells managed game hosting and virtual servers. Game servers and NAT VPS run in Warsaw, Poland (Atman Tier III+). KVM VPS is available in Frankfurt (Germany) and the Netherlands. Protection is always-on via PletX (10+ Tbps filtration capacity). Primary currency in the panel is PLN. Payments: BLIK, Bitcoin (BTC), Litecoin (LTC).
