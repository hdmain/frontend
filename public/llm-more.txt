HOW ORDERING WORKS
1. Customer opens https://cp.alfahost.eu and creates an account (email verification).
2. Wallet top-up: BLIK (Stripe / PLN), Bitcoin, Litecoin.
3. Purchase deducts wallet balance; service appears in the panel.
4. Game servers: pick product, then plan, then engine (runtime), then deploy.
5. VPS NAT: pick plan, then Linux image (Ubuntu/Debian), then root SSH on first mapped port (not 22).
6. VPS KVM DE/NL: pick plan, then OS image (Linux or Windows Server), then public IPv4 plus root/RDP.
7. One open support ticket at a time in the panel; Discord account can be linked for client role and ticket sync.
8. Website purchase buttons go to the panel; opening the panel alone is not an order.

FAQ
Is anti-DDoS free? Yes. Every plan includes multi-layer protection from day one — no activation fee, no paid add-on.
How fast can I deploy? Most game images and VPS templates are ready within seconds after payment clears.
Where are the servers? Game servers and NAT VPS: Warsaw, Poland. KVM VPS: Frankfurt (DE) and the Netherlands. Dedicated: EU.
NAT vs KVM? NAT = shared host IP + fixed 20-port block, Incus/LXC, cheapest. KVM = own public IPv4, full OS including Windows Server options, higher isolation.
